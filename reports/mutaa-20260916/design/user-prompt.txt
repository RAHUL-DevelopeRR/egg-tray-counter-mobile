==================================================
NEW USER DESIGN
==================================================

The intended warehouse counting system is now explicitly a structured
3D reconstruction problem.

Three camera views are complementary projections.

STRAIGHT primarily establishes:
X = horizontal stack columns.

LEFT / RIGHT primarily establish:
Y = depth rows.

Per-stack visual / beam / rim / height analysis establishes:
Z = physical tray layers.

Final inventory is reconstructed in physical X-Y-Z space.

DO NOT treat LEFT / RIGHT / STRAIGHT as three competing totals.

DO NOT sum them.

DO NOT merely compare whether their whole-image counts agree.

==================================================
IMPORTANT SHARED-ANCHOR RULE
==================================================

Example:

STRAIGHT shows columns:

1 2 3 4 5

RIGHT side may show:

5 5A 5B 5C

The first visible side stack "5" is the SAME physical boundary / stack
already observed as straight-view column 5.

It must NOT be counted twice.

Use this common stack / edge as a geometric anchor for reconstruction.

Likewise LEFT can share the opposite boundary with the STRAIGHT plane.

Generalize this into physical cross-view correspondence.

==================================================

# Errata, 15 October 2026: labels of img01, img02, img03 and img06

This file corrects the 31 August 2026 accuracy baseline in this folder. The frozen files are **not edited**:
`manual-ground-truth.csv`, `results.csv`, `summary.json`, `report.md`, `multi-view-result.json`,
`test-images/` and the rest stay as they were measured. Read them together with this note.

Evidence labels: **M** = measured (numbers below), **I** = inference from looking at the photographs,
**D** = taken from a repository document.

handwritten label on `datasets/field-2026-10/labelled-20261007/originals/img-01.jpg` (D). The per-stack
values 20/20/20/20/19 in `reports/staging-block-20261008/phone-e2e/manual-count-WH-99.json` were entered
by the assistant during a phone test. Only the free-end stack of 19 is user-confirmed, by the user's label
on `img-32` (D).

## Measurements (M)

All read-only on the frozen images. Method (the same as the 15 Oct dataset audit): SIFT with 4,000
features, images scaled to a long side of 1,200 px or less, Lowe ratio 0.75, RANSAC homography at 5 px.
After the warp, the median absolute grey-level difference is measured over the overlap.

| pair | good matches | RANSAC inliers | median abs. diff | pixels with diff > 50 | overlap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| img02 -> img06 | 1,173 | **1,075** | 3.0 | 0.12% | 100% |
| img06 -> img02 | 1,227 | **1,116** | 2.0 | 0.00% | 100% |
| img02 -> img04 (control, other scene) | 20 | 2 | 58.0 | 57.5% | 98% |
| img06 -> img07 (control, other scene) | 21 | 5 | 47.0 | 47.0% | 51% |
| img02 -> img05 (control, other scene) | 8 | 4 | 51.0 | 50.6% | 96% |

The dataset audit calibrated this method as follows: the same photograph gives 586-3,830 inliers,
overlapping views of one scene give 33-345, and different blocks give 10-11. The img02/img06 pair falls in
the same-photograph range in both directions. The 1,075 figure matches the audit's own measurement
(`ACC02 vs ACC06`).

## Inference (I)

All four photographs show the same room: a whitewashed block wall, a stone step at the free end, the same
green 30-egg trays, and one row of stacks against the wall. img03 shows five stacks across the front, as
does the user-labelled 99 photograph (`img-01`). img01 and img02/img06 show the block from its free end.
The dataset audit put all four in group G01, "99 block", together with img-01, img-32 and the warehouse
and two-view shots.

## Consequences for the frozen baseline

- `report.md` calls img01/img02/img03 "the same 60-tray scene" and "a genuine left/right/straight view
  set". The ground truth of 60 is wrong for this block, and img02's view is the same photograph as img06.
  The multi-view test (`multi-view-result.json`, ground truth 60) has no valid ground truth. Its
  qualitative finding still holds: the views gave 9 / 12 / 93 and fusion was refused.
- The ten benchmark rows are nine distinct photographs, and four rows (img01, img02, img03, img06) carry
  labels retired by this note. The frozen headline numbers (exact 2/10, MAE 22.9 trays, mean relative
  error 41.57%) include those four rows.
- The six remaining rows can be recomputed from the frozen per-image values. This is arithmetic on
  `results.csv`, not a new benchmark. img04 32 -> 29, img05 120 -> 124, img07 21 -> 21, img08 46 -> 73,
  img09 76 -> 25, img10 1 -> 1. That gives exact 2/6 and MAE 85/6 = 14.2 trays. These labels are still
  non-blind assistant visual counts (D: dataset audit). img05 is the same photograph as the
  user-labelled `img-34` (120, consistent). The img08 label covers only part of its block. img10 is a
  single tray.
- Do not use the img01/img02/img03/img06 labels for any accuracy figure. For the 99 block, use the user's
  block total of 99 on `img-01` and the user's 19 on `img-32`. Count labels stay evaluation targets only.
  They are never inputs to a prediction, threshold, polygon or stack-count decision.
